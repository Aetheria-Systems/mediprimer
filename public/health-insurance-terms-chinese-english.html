Health Insurance Terms in Chinese and English

You hit an English word on a form or a Chinese word from a relative, and you're not sure they mean the same thing. This guide pairs the most common Medicare and Medicaid terms in both languages with a plain-language meaning.

How to use this page. Each row gives the English term you'll see on official forms and websites, the Chinese term (繁體中文) you may hear from family or community sources, and what it actually means. For the two program names people ask about most, see Medicare and Medicaid Explained in Chinese and 白卡 and 紅藍卡. For full definitions of every term on the site, see the Plain-English Glossary.

The programs

EnglishChinese (繁體中文)What it means
Medicare老年醫療保險（紅藍卡）Federal insurance for people 65+ or with certain disabilities, the same in every state.
Medicaid醫療補助（白卡）Joint federal-state coverage for people with low income; rules vary by state.
CHIP兒童健康保險計畫Low-cost or free coverage for children whose family earns too much for Medicaid.
The Marketplace醫療保險市場Where people under 65 without other coverage buy a private plan, often with a subsidy.
Dual eligible雙重資格Qualifying for both Medicare and Medicaid at the same time.
Medicare Savings Program (MSP)醫療保險儲蓄計劃A Medicaid-run program that helps pay Medicare premiums and costs for people with modest income.

Money words

EnglishChinese (繁體中文)What it means
Premium保費The amount you pay, usually monthly, to keep your coverage active.
Deductible自付額What you pay out of pocket before your plan starts paying its share.
Copayment (copay)共付額A fixed dollar amount you pay for a covered service, like $20 for a doctor visit.
Coinsurance共同保險Your percentage share of a bill after the deductible is met, like 20%.
Extra Help額外補助A program that lowers Part D prescription drug premiums and costs for people with low income.
Premium tax credit保費稅收抵免A subsidy that lowers the monthly cost of a Marketplace plan based on income.

Enrollment words

EnglishChinese (繁體中文)What it means
Open Enrollment公開註冊期A yearly window when anyone can sign up for or change coverage.
Special Enrollment Period (SEP)特別註冊期A window to enroll outside Open Enrollment, triggered by a life change like losing other coverage.
Beneficiary受益人Someone who receives benefits from a program like Medicare or Medicaid.
Renewal / Redetermination續保 / 重新審核資格The periodic check that confirms you still qualify for Medicaid or CHIP.
Terms, not translations of legal documents. These are plain-language explanations to help you recognize a term, not official translations. When you apply for a program or sign a form, use the agency's own translated materials or a qualified interpreter — many state Medicaid agencies and Medicare provide free interpreter services. Common questions

Are 白卡 and 紅藍卡 official program names?

No. They are community nicknames based on the physical card colors. The official names are Medicaid (醫療補助) and Medicare (老年醫療保險). See 白卡 and 紅藍卡: What Medicaid and Medicare Actually Mean.

Can I get an interpreter when I apply for these programs?

Yes. Medicare and state Medicaid agencies are generally required to provide free interpreter services for people who need them. See Medical Interpreter Rights for how to request one.
