What Medicare Costs in 2026

One page with the numbers people actually ask about. Print it, keep it on the fridge, or send it to a parent. Every figure comes from medicare.gov and is dated, so you can tell at a glance whether it is current.

What Medicare Costs in 2026

The standard amounts. Your costs can differ if your income is higher, if you have a Medicare Advantage or Medigap plan, or if you get help paying.

Part A — Hospital

Monthly premium$0 for most people
If you have to buy Part A$311 or $565 a month if you have to buy it
Hospital deductible$1,736 per benefit period
Hospital days 61–90$434 a day
Lifetime reserve days$868 a day
Skilled nursing facility$217 a day (days 21–100)

Part B — Doctors and outpatient care

Monthly premium$202.90 a month (more if your income is higher)
Yearly deductible$283 a year
Your share after the deductibleUsually 20% of the approved amount

Part D — Prescription drugs

Most you pay out of pocket$2,100 a year, then you pay nothing more for covered drugs
Highest deductible a plan may chargeNo plan may charge more than $615
National base premium$38.99 a month (used to figure late penalties)

Figures for 2026 from medicare.gov. If your income is limited, programs may pay these for you — see mediprimer.org/getting-help.html. Free help: 1-800-MEDICARE or your SHIP counselor.
