Your Medicare Annual Review Workbook

Open enrollment runs October 15 to December 7. Work through this once and you will know whether to stay or switch — and why.

Most people never open the notice their plan sends in September, renew by doing nothing, and find out in January that a drug moved tiers or a doctor left the network. This workbook walks you through the same checks a counselor would make. Fill it in on screen — your answers stay in this browser and are never sent anywhere — or print it blank and use a pen.

Need this year's standard figures while you fill this in? Keep the printable cost card beside you.

What to do with it once it's filled in

  • Nothing looks worse? You can do nothing; most plans renew automatically. Keep the workbook — next year's comparison starts here.
  • A drug is dropping off the list or moving tiers? That is the most common reason to switch. Compare plans with your drug list in Medicare's Plan Finder.
  • A doctor is leaving the network? Check whether the plan you are considering includes them for next year — not this year.
  • The numbers went up a lot? If your income is limited, you may qualify for help you are not getting — see Getting Help Paying.
  • Want a person? Your SHIP counselor will go through this with you free of charge.

Open enrollment ends December 7. We'll remind you.

Get our fall review checklist — the short list of things to check on your plan before the deadline — plus one reminder email before December 7. Nothing else, no ads, no plan sales.

We'll email you once to confirm — you're not subscribed until you click it. A reminder before the deadline, then our usual one-email-a-week-at-most. Unsubscribe in one click.

The dates that matter

Your plan's Annual Notice of Change arrives by the end of September — see what to look for in it. Open enrollment runs October 15 to December 7, and anything you change then starts January 1. If you are in a Medicare Advantage plan and change your mind, you get one more switch between January 1 and March 31.

Full detail in Medicare Open Enrollment, and the year-round view in Enrollment & Deadlines.

This workbook is a free organising tool, not advice about which plan to choose. MediPrimer sells nothing and recommends no plan. Confirm details with your plan, Medicare, or your SHIP before you act.
